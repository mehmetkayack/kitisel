## Main features of the service

Kitisel is a software service that analyzes your site, suggests topics, writes blog articles with AI, publishes them on your site with your approval or according to the automatic setting you choose, and reports results. The scope of each plan (monthly articles, number of sites, update suggestions, tracked keywords, team members) is listed on the [Pricing](/pricing) page.

## Total price

The total amount including taxes for the chosen plan and billing interval is shown on the checkout page before you confirm. Prices are in US dollars (USD). Each payment covers the chosen period (one month or one year); renewal is your choice and there are no automatic charges. Any price change is announced before renewal.

## Payment and performance

Payment is taken from a credit or debit card through PayTR, or by bank transfer to our bank account shown on the checkout page. For card payments and transfers to the TRY account, the amount is converted to Turkish lira at the CBRT selling rate at the time of the order and shown before you pay. The service is activated in your account as soon as a card payment is confirmed, or when a bank transfer reaches our account, and is provided electronically. There is no physical delivery.

## Term and termination

Plans are sold in monthly or yearly periods and do not renew automatically. We send you a renewal reminder before the period ends; if you don't renew, nothing more is charged and the service continues until the end of the paid period.

## Right of withdrawal

There is no right of withdrawal for services performed instantly in an electronic environment (Distance Contracts Regulation Art. 15/1-ğ). The Seller refunds the full first payment without conditions if requested within 14 days of the first payment. Details: [Cancellation, Refund and Delivery Policy](/legal/refund).
