Last updated: 12 October 2026

## Subject, duration, nature and purpose

The Processor processes personal data to provide the service (site analysis, content creation, publishing, reporting) and only on the Controller's documented instructions (service settings and use). The duration is the term of the service agreement.

- Names and email addresses of team members, activity logs.

Processing of special categories of personal data is not intended; the Controller agrees not to upload such data to the service.

## Processor obligations

- Processes data only on documented instructions and informs the Controller if it believes an instruction is unlawful.
- Ensures that personnel with access to the data are bound by confidentiality.
- Implements appropriate technical and organizational measures: TLS in transit, AES-256-GCM encryption of credentials, argon2id password hashing, data separation between organizations, role-based access, audit logs of administrative actions.
- Reasonably assists the Controller in responding to data subject requests.
- Notifies the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.
- Deletes the data at the end of the agreement, subject to legal retention obligations, and provides an export before deletion on request.
- Makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this agreement.
