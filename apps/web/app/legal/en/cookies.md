# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Cookies are small files saved in your browser when you visit a site. Kitisel ({{site}}) **only uses cookies that are strictly necessary for the service to work**. We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, so we don't ask for cookie consent. If we add an analytics tool in the future, we won't load it without your explicit consent and we will update this page.

## Cookies we use

- **upvine_rt:** keeps you signed in (refresh token). Readable only by the server (httpOnly), sent only over secure connections. Lifetime: 30 days or until you sign out.
- **upvine_csrf:** protects session actions against forged requests. Lifetime: same as the session.
- **upvine_oauth:** security check during Sign in with Google. Lifetime: 10 minutes.
- **upvine_locale:** remembers your interface language. Lifetime: 1 year.
- **upvine_theme:** remembers your light/dark theme choice. Lifetime: 1 year.

## Third-party cookies

- **Cloudflare Turnstile:** if the bot check is enabled on the free analysis form, Cloudflare may use cookies needed for the check.
- **Sign in with Google:** when you click “Continue with Google”, you are redirected to Google, where Google's own cookie policy applies.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, you may not be able to sign in or use the app.
